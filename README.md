Supertime is an immersive Minecraft mod inspired by the mechanics of the iconic game SUPERHOT. This modification completely transforms your survival experience by binding the flow of in-game time directly to your movements.

When you stand completely still, the world freezes entirely. But the moment you turn, step forward, or enter combat, time springs back to life.

How Time Dilation Works: 0.0x (Total Freeze): You are completely stationary (no keyboard input, camera is not moving). Everything in the world pauses.

0.1x (Focus): You are looking around with the mouse/camera without using movement keys.

0.3x (Sneaking): You are moving while holding Shift.

0.7x (Walking): You are moving at a normal walking pace.

1.0x (Full Speed): You are sprinting or jumping.

Features: Total World Synchronization: The slowdown affects everything globally—including mob AI and movement, skeleton arrows, snowballs, falling blocks (like sand and gravel), item drop physics, and player animations.

Smooth Client-Side Rendering: Built with advanced frame interpolation fixes to ensure mob models and environment pieces don't stutter, warp, or tear when time stops.

Tactical Combat: Turn chaotic monster hordes into a puzzle of positioning, reflexes, and precision.
